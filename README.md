The weekly impressions trend reveals a consistent distribution across the four ad formats over the 15-week period analyzed.

Image ads ranked second, recording approximately 4,389 impressions in Week 19 before increasing to around 6,773 impressions throughout Weeks 20 to 31, followed by a significant decrease to 2,498 impressions in Week 32.

Overall, all four ad formats followed a very similar pattern, characterized by a gradual increase in impressions during the middle weeks followed by a noticeable decline in the final week of the analysis. Among them, Stories consistently generated the highest number of impressions, while Video ads remained the lowest-performing format.


The age distribution shows that impressions were concentrated among younger audiences. The highest number of impressions was recorded for 16-year-olds, with approximately 19,858 impressions, followed by a sharp decline at age 17 to around 8,566 impressions.

From ages 18 to 20, impressions increased again, reaching approximately 15,007 impressions at age 20. Between ages 21 and 29, impressions remained relatively stable, averaging around 15,500 impressions, with only minor fluctuations.

Beginning at age 30, impressions showed a gradual downward trend, decreasing from approximately 12,922 impressions to around 1,976 impressions by age 43. A slight increase was observed at ages 44 and 45, reaching approximately 2,350 impressions, before declining once again to around 1,261 impressions by age 50.

Overall, the results indicate that impressions were concentrated primarily among younger audiences, particularly individuals in their late teens and twenties. Engagement gradually decreased with age, suggesting that the advertising campaigns reached younger demographics more frequently than older ones.


* #### Hourly Trend, Ad Type Analysis & Country
  
<p align="center">

To analyze short-term performance, impressions were examined over a 24-hour period. Overall, impressions remained relatively stable throughout the day, with only moderate fluctuations between the highest and lowest values.

The first peak occurred at 2:00 AM, reaching approximately 14,324 impressions, followed by a slight decline to around 14,064 impressions at 4:00 AM. Impressions increased again at 5:00 AM to approximately 14,183, before reaching the day's lowest point at 7:00 AM, with 13,971 impressions.

Performance recovered immediately afterward, with the second-highest peak recorded at 8:00 AM (14,337 impressions). Following a brief decline at 9:00 AM, impressions increased once more, reaching the highest value of the day at 11:00 AM, with approximately 14,346 impressions. From 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, impressions remained relatively stable at around 14,240 impressions, before decreasing slightly at 3:00 PM.

Between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM, impressions gradually increased again, peaking at approximately 14,193 impressions, followed by a minor decline at 7:00 PM. Activity then increased during 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM, remaining relatively stable before dropping again at 10:00 PM. The day concluded with a slight recovery during the final hour.

Overall, the hourly distribution exhibited only minor fluctuations, indicating that impressions remained relatively consistent throughout the day.

From a geographic perspective, the United States generated the highest number of impressions, with approximately 97,336, followed by the United Kingdom (48,965) and Germany (26,980).

These results indicate that the advertising campaigns achieved their greatest reach within these markets. While countries such as India have a larger overall Facebook user base, campaign performance depends on the selected target audience and advertising strategy rather than the total number of platform users. Therefore, the observed distribution reflects the campaign's targeting rather than overall Facebook popularity by country.

Across the different ad formats, Video ads delivered the strongest overall performance, recording the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) (11.90%), Engagement Rate (ER) (13.74%), and a highly competitive Conversion Rate (CR) (5.07%). Stories generated the highest number of impressions (108,932) while also achieving the highest Purchase Rate (PR) (0.63%) and Conversion Rate (CR) (5.34%).

Overall, the results suggest that Stories were the most effective format for maximizing audience reach and conversions, whereas Video ads excelled at generating user engagement. Image and Carousel ads produced solid results across all performance metrics but generally ranked below Stories and Video, indicating opportunities to prioritize higher-performing formats depending on campaign objectives.

Instagram recorded a total of 123,840 impressions during the period analyzed, generating over 14,690 clicks, 682 shares, more than 1,500 comments, approximately 708 purchases, and a total of 17,000 engagements.

The platform achieved a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 11.86%, while the Engagement Rate reached 13.60%. In terms of conversions, the Conversion Rate was 4.82%, with a Purchase Rate of 0.57%. Overall, the advertising campaigns were supported by a total budget of approximately $227 million, averaging $5 million per campaign.
